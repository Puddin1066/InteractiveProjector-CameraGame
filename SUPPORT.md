# Support

- Setup help and bugs: open a [bug report](https://github.com/Puddin1066/InteractiveProjector-CameraGame/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  Include your camera, projector, operating system and what `python3 -m tools.camera_probe` prints.
- Ideas and new games: open a [feature request](https://github.com/Puddin1066/InteractiveProjector-CameraGame/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- Security problems: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

Start with the [README](README.md), whose troubleshooting section covers the
usual camera, projector and calibration problems.
